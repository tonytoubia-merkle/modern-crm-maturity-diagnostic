# Content Supply Chain Diagnostic — Questions & Answer Options

> **Source of truth:** `lib/csc/data/questions.ts` on `claude/review-supply-chain-diagnostic-Wo1Pm`.
> **Entry point:** `/csc` → `/csc/assessment/new` → `/csc/results/[shareId]`.
> **Source questionnaire:** `Content Supply Chain Diagnostic.xlsx` (Microsoft Forms export, 45 maturity questions).

The diagnostic is a **45-question core assessment** across **6 capability dimensions**, plus **5 optional industry questions** for one of 5 industries. Every question uses the same 5-point maturity scale.

Each core question includes a **Level-5 (Optimized) anchor** drawn directly from the xlsx source, so respondents can calibrate against the intended maturity rubric. Where the xlsx contained observed sample answers, those are listed underneath.

---

## Maturity Scale (applies to every question)

| Score | Label | Description |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | Not in Place | Capability does not exist or is highly fragmented with no formal process. |
| **2** | Emerging | Limited pilots or isolated capabilities exist but are not consistently applied. |
| **3** | Operational | Capability is in use and operational but not consistently integrated across teams, brands, or channels. |
| **4** | Integrated | Capability operates across teams, brands, and channels with clear governance and coordination. |
| **5** | Optimized | Capability is fully orchestrated, continuously improved through data and AI, and drives measurable content outcomes. |

---

## Maturity Stages (overall roll-up)

| Overall Score | Stage | Label |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| < 1.75 | Stage 1 | Campaign-Driven Content |
| 1.75 – 2.74 | Stage 2 | Process-Based Content |
| 2.75 – 3.74 | Stage 3 | Connected Content Supply Chain |
| 3.75 – 5.0 | Stage 4 | Intelligent Content Engine |

# Core Questions (45)

## 1. Strategy & Planning — Strategic Foundation

*Assess the extent to which content strategy, ideation, portfolio planning, editorial calendars, and content policies are designed to deliver audience-led, outcomes-driven content at scale.*

### Q1
**To what extent is content strategy developed with clear integration to business objectives, audience insights, and measurable outcomes — rather than planned around product launches or calendar cadence?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Content planning is dynamic and continuously optimized based on audience insights, performance analytics, and real-time data.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Content strategies begin to align with broader business objectives
- Content strategies are consistently integrated with measurable goals tied to audience needs and business outcomes
- Content planning is dynamic and continuously optimized based on audience insights, performance analytics, and real-time data

### Q2
**To what extent is the approach to updating existing content structured, data-driven, and increasingly automated — rather than reactive or manual?**

**Level 5 anchor:** AI-powered systems predict refresh needs and auto-prioritize updates based on performance signals.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- No structured process for refreshing or updating content; updates occur reactively
- Standardized workflows refresh core content types based on basic metrics
- Automated tools flag outdated content using metrics
- AI-powered systems predict refresh needs and auto-prioritize updates

### Q3
**To what extent is a consistent look and feel maintained across content types through enforced governance, automation, and monitoring tools?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Enterprise-wide adoption of a robust governance framework with advanced tools for automation and monitoring.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Some basic awareness of the need for content governance, but implementation is fragmented
- A partially documented governance framework is established and covers basic policies and standards
- A comprehensive governance structure is documented and actively implemented across the organization
- Enterprise-wide adoption of a robust governance framework with advanced tools for automation and monitoring

### Q4
**To what extent are new content ideas generated systematically across channels — including through AI-powered ideation from brand-aligned sources?**

**Level 5 anchor:** A unified AI-powered platform generates a vast number of on-brand content ideas from a single prompt.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Manual brainstorms which are time-consuming and often result in limited ideas that are based on individual experiences
- A mix of disparate tools is used for each channel, leading to inconsistent brand messaging
- Integrated system where creative tools can access brand assets
- Unified AI powered platform allows a vast number of on brand content ideas to be generated from a single prompt

### Q5
**To what extent are content ideas assessed for potential performance before production — using predictive analytics and AI content analytics?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Predictive campaign and AI content analytics validate ideas and assess potential effectiveness across audiences.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Focus groups give helpful feedback, but it is time consuming and inefficient for high levels of content
- Using analytics and historical data to help assess potential of ideas
- Predictive campaign and AI content analytics to validate ideas and assess potential effectiveness against different audiences

### Q6
**To what extent do teams collaborate around a shared, dynamic content portfolio plan aligned to business goals, audience insights, and campaign priorities?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Enterprise-wide adoption of a dynamic content portfolio planning framework.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- A documented content portfolio plan exists and covers key themes and priorities for content creation
- A centralized content portfolio plan is fully documented and aligned with strategic business goals, audience insights, and campaign priorities
- Enterprise-wide adoption of a dynamic content portfolio planning framework

### Q7
**To what extent is the content calendar centralized in content management platforms and dynamically optimized — ideally with AI-driven scheduling?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Enterprise-wide adoption of a sophisticated editorial calendar integrated with AI-driven scheduling tools.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Basic editorial calendar is used sporadically generally in spreadsheets or ad hoc tools
- A centralized editorial calendar is established and used by most teams
- Editorial calendar is fully integrated into a centralized content management platform
- Enterprise-wide adoption of a sophisticated editorial calendar system integrated with advanced tools like AI-driven scheduling

### Q8
**To what extent are content production policies and standards documented, enforced, and continuously updated based on performance and regulatory changes?**

**Level 5 anchor:** Policies and standards are continuously updated based on performance analytics and regulatory changes across the full content lifecycle.

*Observed answers in the xlsx source:*
- Teams are beginning to align their workflows with governance guidelines, but adherence is inconsistent
- Policies and standards cover all aspects of the content lifecycle across creation, management, and distribution
- Policies and standards are continuously updated based on performance analytics and regulatory changes
